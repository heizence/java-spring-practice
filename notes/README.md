# Your notes

This folder is yours. Two habits are worth keeping:

1. **One short note per concept, in your own words.** For example `event-loop-vs-threads.md` or `equals-and-hashcode.md`. Write it after an exercise, without looking at the lesson. If you can't explain something simply, you've found what to review.
2. **A "looked it up" log**: [lookup-log.md](lookup-log.md). Every time you search for syntax or an API, add a line. Anything that appears twice goes on a flashcard or into a short drill. Over the weeks, the log should get shorter.
